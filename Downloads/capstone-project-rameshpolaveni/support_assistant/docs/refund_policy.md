# Returns and Refunds

Customers may return eligible products within 7 days of delivery if the item is unopened, undamaged, and still within the original packaging.

Refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days once the return is approved and the item is received by Zepto.

Perishable items, opened food products, and custom or personalized goods are generally excluded from the return window unless there is a quality issue or a delivery error.
