# Shipping and Delivery Policy

Zepto aims to deliver groceries and household items within 24 to 48 hours for eligible metro locations.

Orders placed before 8:00 PM local time are generally dispatched the same day. Delivery windows may shift for weather disruptions, high order volume, or local operational constraints.

For customers in remote or rural areas, delivery may take longer and is subject to an updated ETA. Customers should check the order status page for the latest delivery window.
